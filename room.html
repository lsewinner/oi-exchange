EXCHANGE ROOM / PROJECT PROCUREMENT

From intent to agreement.

A procurement scenario in which a requirement becomes work packages, invitations, structured proposals and a human-recorded decision.

Fictional scenario · local simulation
Demonstration only. Organisations, proposals and activity are fictional. No invitations are sent, no AI agents run, and no external commitments are made. You can operate every step yourself; this is not identity verification, an accepted quote, a contract or a payment. Entries remain in this browser's localStorage only. Do not enter sensitive or real procurement data.

    WHAT COMES NEXT

    Real collaboration requires real boundaries.

    Before an institution can use this for real work: organisation identity, scoped invitations, server-side permissions, proposal versioning, immutable-enough event provenance and explicit human approvals must be implemented and tested.
