    03 / TRUST & DECISIONS

    Who can decide?

    Anyone with this browser can change roles and read all entries. Role-specific buttons only illustrate a proposed permission model; they do not enforce real authorisation.

    Human decision gate

    A buyer may simulate selecting one proposal per work package. Check its basis and conditions first. This does not award a contract, accept a binding quote, send a message or make a payment.

    Not implemented

    Verified identities, real invitations, independent agent connections, Oxford Bot integration, contracting, payment and trusted audit records require a secure backend and separate authorisation.
