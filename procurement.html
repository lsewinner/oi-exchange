INTERACTIVE DEMO / PROCUREMENT

From requirement to selection.

Create a demand, invite fictional suppliers, switch roles to submit proposals, then make a documented buyer selection. No account or real supplier communication is involved.

Local simulation only: anyone can switch roles, names are not verified, invitations are not sent and selections are not contracts or actual purchasing decisions. Data remains in this browser’s localStorage; do not enter sensitive information. Refresh to inspect what was saved.
