One opportunity. Several possible partners. A decision that stays with a person.

Fictional organisations · no live transactions
This is a local prototype, not a live procurement service. All organisations and opportunities below are fictional. Anyone can switch roles in this browser; this does not verify identity, authority or legal approval. Invites and proposals are not sent, prices are examples, and approval does not create a contract or payment. Data stays in this browser’s localStorage. Do not enter confidential information.
