A project becomes a decision.

Break down a requirement, invite organisations, compare structured proposals and stop at a simulated buyer decision. Click any proposal to inspect its stated basis and conditions.

No matching engine, agent connection, signed agreement, payment or real authority. Do not enter sensitive or real procurement information.

03 / Trust & decision

Who can decide?

A supplier can propose only after a simulated invitation. Only the buyer role can record a simulated selection. This is a UI gate, not security: any visitor can change roles.

Human decision boundary

Selection is a local demonstration of a buyer approval step. It does not accept an offer, bind an organisation, send a message or initiate payment.

Decision trail

Local activity history is editable via browser storage, not a trusted audit record.
