01 / WHY IT EXISTS

Not AI without boundaries.
Collaboration with boundaries.

Project teams rarely lack messages. What they often lack are rules everyone understands. This prototype puts rules and key decisions before the handoff: what can be shared, what completion means and who checks it.

01 / AGREEMENT

Start with a shared agreement

Record both parties, the goal, permitted sharing and acceptance criteria. Once an agreement changes, pending requests under the previous version can no longer proceed.

02 / HUMAN REVIEW

People decide what happens next

Check the sharing scope before requesting a handoff. The recipient gives a reason when approving or rejecting it in the demo; the system does not automatically allow it through.

03 / TRACEABILITY

Show the process without overstating it

The browser displays a local record of actions to illustrate the decision trail. It is not a trusted audit system, e-signature or real permissions framework.

02 / PROCUREMENT DEMO

From demand to selection,
one path you can try.

Try a browser-only procurement scenario without signing up. Act as a buyer to describe a requirement and invite suppliers, then switch to a simulated supplier to submit a proposal. Return to the buyer role to record a selection and its reason. No invitations are sent and no purchasing decision is made.

STEP 01 / DEMAND & INVITATION

Define the need

STEP 02 / PROPOSAL

STEP 03 / SELECTION

Document the decision

Compare the saved proposals and record which one was chosen and why. The result stays in this browser.
